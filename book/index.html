    This Review Is Built for Families Like Yours

    It will be most useful if:

    • Your household earns about $250,000 or more, or pays about $30,000 or more a year in federal taxes.
    • You expect to help pay for college.
    • You have college costs coming up or already underway.
    • You have savings, investments, retirement contributions, or annual cash flow that should be looked at together.
    • You want to weigh suitability, risk, liquidity, timing, and tax consequences before acting.

    It's Probably Not a Fit If

    • You don't expect to help pay for college.
    • You're looking for guaranteed tax savings, investment returns, cash flow, or financial aid.
    • You want to act on a tax or investment idea without professional review.
